# 약 봉투, AI한테 물어보세요 — Treatment

Style: Mascot Screencast (`living-screencast`). 60 s, vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps. Korean narration (edge-tts ko-KR-HyunsuMultilingualNeural, the newest and most natural Korean voice, +4–10 % per line so each fits its scene), the senior's question in a lowered SunHi voice (a mother asking). Burned-in caption pills + `subtitles_ko.srt`.

Source: the script and direction guide written earlier in this conversation. Adapted to the style: no hands or faces on screen. The **touch dot is the user**, and an original **pixel mascot is the AI**. The app is a generic "AI 도우미" chat app (no real logos or UI). ChatGPT and Gemini are named only in the narration, as examples.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One take inside one phone** — the phone floats on a warm wallpaper; the camera pushes into whatever is being touched; the four steps are four chapters. Clear, closest to how seniors will actually see it.
2. **Before / after split** — left: squinting at the envelope with a magnifier; right: asking the phone. Strong, but two halves of a 1080-wide frame make every UI element half as big — wrong for this audience.
3. **The mascot's errand** — the mascot carries the question from the mic into the photo, reads the rows, and brings the answer back. Charming, but the steps the viewer must copy get less screen time.

**Chosen: 1, with 3's errand as the step ④ beat.** Seniors need the button they must press to be huge and alone on screen; a single phone with push-ins does that. The mascot reading the photo row by row (from 3) makes "the AI reads it for you" visible in one second.

## 2. Logline and arc

A tiny-print medicine envelope that nobody can read becomes a big, plain answer after three taps and one spoken question.

- Setup (0–18 s): tiny print, too many envelopes, not wanting to bother the children. The mascot pops out of the phone's camera hole: "the phone reads it for you".
- Turn (18–41 s): ① open the app → ② snap the envelope → ③ hold the mic and ask "이 약 어떻게 먹어?" → ④ the mascot runs across the photo's rows and the answer arrives in big type. A follow-up question shows that asking again is fine.
- Ending (41–60 s): the four steps as four tiles, a reminder notification for tomorrow's hospital visit, subscribe, and share with parents. The mascot rides the shared message out of frame.

## 3. Benchmark

- Phone-tutorial shorts for older viewers (large-type "how to" channels) and Apple-style guided tours. **Learn**: one action per sentence, the button circled before it is pressed, everything three times bigger than a normal UI. **Don't take**: their UI, mascots, music or layouts.
- Duolingo-style mascot acting. **Learn**: anticipation and squash before every jump; eyes look at what matters. **Don't take**: the character or its design.

## 6. Cue map (version 2: shorts energy)

120 BPM in A minor (Am F C G), all synthesised: four-on-the-floor kick, claps on 2 and 4, offbeat hats, a pumping saw bass ducked by the kick, chord stabs on the offbeats. The mascot's square-wave lead plays a two-bar hook (E A C A E D C · D C A G A…), repeated so it sticks. Impact hits on 0.0, 18.2 (the drop into step ①), 32.9 (the answer) and 41.6 (the summary); risers into 18.2 and 41.6; clap fills before steps ② and ③. The problem section (7.2–18.2) is low-passed with half-time kicks and no lead; everything drops out while the AI reads (31.95–32.9). Music sits about 8 dB under the voice and ducks under every line. Camera punches (a 4.5 % zoom bump) on the hook words.

## 8. Text design

Noto Sans KR (OFL), self-hosted and subset. UI type 40–64 px inside the phone (three times a normal phone UI, for older eyes). Version 2 palette: violet → hot-pink → orange wallpaper with white stripes and yellow rings, a hot-pink mascot, an electric-blue AI app, yellow highlights. Caption pill at the bottom: near-black, 60 px heavy white type with key words in yellow, a small pop-in, at most two lines, never over the element being touched. Step pill at the top (yellow, black outline): "① 앱 열기 / ② 사진 찍기 / ③ 마이크로 묻기 / ④ 답 듣기". A fixed safety line from 33 s to 41 s: "※ 정확한 복용법은 약사·의사와 꼭 확인하세요". Envelope, patient and pharmacy names are fictional ("튼튼약국", "김○○ 님").
